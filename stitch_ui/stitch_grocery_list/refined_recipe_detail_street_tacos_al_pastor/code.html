Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Pork shoulder, thinly sliced

    3 dried Guajillo chilies
  • 2 tbsp Achiote paste
  • 1/2 cup Pineapple juice
  • 1 Fresh pineapple, sliced
  • Corn tortillas

Chef's Secret

format_quote

Use fresh pineapple juice for the marinade—the enzymes act as a natural tenderizer that makes the pork incredibly succulent.

Method

01

The Marinade

Rehydrate the chilies in boiling water for 15 minutes. Blend with achiote paste, pineapple juice, vinegar, and garlic until completely smooth. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve for a silky, refined texture.

02

Preparation

Coat the sliced pork thinly and evenly with the marinade. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, though overnight is ideal to allow the flavors to penetrate deeply.

03

Char & Sear

Heat a large cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking. Sear the meat in small batches to avoid crowding, ensuring crispy charred edges. Grill pineapple slices simultaneously until caramelized.

04

Assembly

Serve on doubled-up warm corn tortillas. Top with the charred pineapple, finely chopped white onion, and fresh cilantro. Serve with plenty of lime wedges.
