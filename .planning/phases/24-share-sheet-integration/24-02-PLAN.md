  <what-built>
    Complete share sheet integration: expo-share-intent configured with iOS URL activation rules, +native-intent.ts routes share intents to add-recipe screen, ShareIntentProvider wraps app, add-recipe auto-imports shared URLs.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Test all three success criteria on a real iOS device:

    **SHARE-01: App appears in share sheet**
    1. Open Safari and navigate to a YouTube video URL
    2. Tap the Share button
    3. Verify "SharedKitchen" appears in the share sheet app list
    4. Repeat from TikTok app (share a cooking video)
    5. Repeat from Instagram app (share a Reel)

    **SHARE-02: Tapping opens app and begins import**
    1. From YouTube, share a cooking video URL to SharedKitchen
    2. Verify the app opens to the Add Recipe screen
    3. Verify the URL is pre-filled in the import field
    4. Verify the video import process starts automatically (progress messages appear)
    5. Verify the extracted recipe preview appears after processing

    **SHARE-03: Works in all app states**
    1. With SharedKitchen in the FOREGROUND: Share a URL from Safari -- verify import starts
    2. With SharedKitchen in the BACKGROUND: Share a URL from TikTok -- verify app comes to foreground and import starts
    3. Force-quit SharedKitchen (swipe away), then share a URL -- verify app launches fresh and import starts

    **Edge case: Non-video URL**
    1. Share a regular article URL (e.g., allrecipes.com recipe page) from Safari
    2. Verify it opens add-recipe and falls through to the HTML import pipeline (not video pipeline)
